Kühtai, competition day

The line I did not ski

At Kühtai I found a line on the right side of the face that nobody else had seen. My coach said he would never have spotted it, and that it was my call. Skied cleanly, I believed it could win.

There was one rock in the landing.

By the next morning I had taken the whole face apart. Then I had to pick a side.

LeftI chose left. On my last jump a slide of snow took my vision. I landed blind and crashed.

SlopeShare, started April 2026

The bus that did not stop

On the way to the resorts I kept watching full buses and trams drive past people who were waiting.

SlopeShare is my answer for now. It is a ride-sharing platform for people travelling to Austrian ski resorts. Friends have tested it and it works. It is still in beta and not public yet.

I am not trying to make money from it. The real goal is better public transport to the mountains.
